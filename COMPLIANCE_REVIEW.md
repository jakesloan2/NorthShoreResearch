# Pre-launch compliance review

This static-site update is a copy and structure review, not legal, regulatory or tax advice. It deliberately does not claim that the site or products are compliant.

## Do not launch until confirmed

- Replace every `[PLACEHOLDER]` with the trading entity's legal name, company number, registered address, contact email, return address, privacy contact, website domain and (if applicable) VAT details.
- Obtain specialist UK legal and regulatory advice on the classification, import, marketing, sale and supply of every listed material. Research-use-only wording does not itself determine regulatory status or make supply lawful.
- Verify every molecular formula, product description, SKU, size, stock level, price, certificate, batch-test badge and label against the underlying supplier documentation. This update preserves the supplied catalogue data and does not independently validate it.
- Confirm that the documentation-request process can supply only genuine, relevant records and that no documentation, testing, purity or batch claim is made unless it can be evidenced.
- Configure a real payment provider and server-side order capture. The current `demo` payment mode does not take payment, create an order, send an email or retain an auditable order record.
- Configure a real contact-form endpoint; the current contact form only shows a success toast and does not send the message.
- Finalise delivery, dispatch, returns, refunds and cancellation terms against the actual fulfilment process. Do not retain the default gym collection, delivery charge or timing text if unavailable.
- Complete UK GDPR/PECR work: identify all processors, retention periods, international transfers and lawful bases; configure consent before enabling analytics, marketing, newsletter collection or tracking pixels.
- Confirm age-gating, customer screening, restricted-product controls, safe handling, storage, packaging, shipping and record-keeping requirements with qualified advisers.

## Copy decisions in this update

- Catalogue names, sizes, prices, molecular-formula strings and supplied research descriptions were retained rather than invented or independently “corrected”.
- Sports-nutrition and food-supplement language was removed from core navigation, homepage, footer, terms, disclaimer, returns and quality copy because it conflicts with the current research-compound catalogue.
- Unsupported universal claims about testing, dispatch, tracking, payment methods and returns were softened or removed.
