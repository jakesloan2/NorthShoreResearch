Quality & documentation

How we present catalogue information and documentation requests.

Product information is presented for laboratory and in-vitro research context only. It is not medical, veterinary, dosing or administration advice.

Product information

Each product page shows the supplied catalogue name, listed size, molecular-formula information and research description. This information should be verified against the relevant product and supplier documentation before reliance or use.

Documentation and COAs

Product documentation and certificates of analysis are available on request, subject to the relevant product, batch and verification. Requesting a document does not confirm its availability or make a testing, purity or suitability claim.

When requesting documentation, provide the product name, listed size and batch number where available. Any document supplied should be matched to the relevant product and batch.

Research-use boundary

Materials are supplied solely for lawful laboratory, research and analytical purposes. They are not offered for human or veterinary use, consumption, administration, food, cosmetics, medicines or medical devices.
