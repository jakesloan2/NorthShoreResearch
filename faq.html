FAQ

Research materials, documentation, ordering and support.

Research-use-only position

Are these products for human or veterinary use?
No. North Shore Research supplies materials solely for laboratory and in-vitro research. They are not for human or veterinary use, consumption, administration, food, cosmetics, medicines or medical devices.
Do you provide dosing or administration advice?
No. We do not provide dosing, administration, clinical, veterinary or therapeutic advice. Product descriptions are supplied for research context only.
Can I use the materials in a research project?
You must confirm that your proposed purchase, possession, handling and intended use are lawful, appropriate and authorised for your organisation before ordering.

Documentation

How do I request documentation or a COA?
Product documentation and certificates of analysis are available on request, subject to the relevant product, batch and verification. Contact us with the product and batch details where available. A request does not confirm that a document is available or make any testing claim.
What product information is shown?
Each product page displays the supplied catalogue name, listed size, molecular-formula information and research description. Verify all information against the applicable supplier documentation before use.

Orders and support

How are delivery and dispatch handled?
Available delivery options and charges are shown at checkout. Dispatch timing is confirmed after order review; do not rely on a dispatch estimate until that confirmation.
What is the returns policy?
See the returns policy before ordering. Opened, unsealed, altered or handled materials are not eligible for a change-of-mind return unless required by law.
How can I contact North Shore Research?
Use the details on our contact page for product-documentation or order queries.
